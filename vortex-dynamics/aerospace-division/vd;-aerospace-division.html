F/A-80

The prototype airframe, It could barely fly when it was first made but after a few different variations it proved itself and production was started.

F/A-80A

The single seater variant of the airframe, It only has a singular pilot. Has a slightly higher fuel capacity than the twin seater variant

F/A-80B

The twin seater version of the airframe, it has pilot and a WSO, Has a slightly lower fuel capacity then the single seater variant.

The F/A-80 was then made into two nearly identical airframes with the only difference being one is a twin seater and one is a solo seater variant.

F/A-80T

The trainer variant, it's used to teach people the basic controls of the airframe. Used to teach people all F/A-80 Airframes.
